Privacy

How this website handles your information

Effective October 18, 2026

What we collect

This website has no forms, no accounts, no tracking cookies and no advertising. We don't collect personal information when you browse it.

When you email us

If you email support@yokefellowai.com, we receive your message and email address and use them only to reply and to arrange a discovery call. We don't sell or share them, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services this site relies on

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations.
  • Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • Email: messages to yokefellowai.com addresses are forwarded by our domain provider to our inbox.

Each of these providers handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Client work

Client installations run on hardware and accounts the client owns. How client data is handled is set out in each client's written agreement.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: support@yokefellowai.com.
