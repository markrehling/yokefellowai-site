Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You text your workforce the way you'd text an employee. I handle the installation, the security and the training.

Will it send anything on its own?

No. Every outbound email, post or message waits for your approval by text. The workforce prepares; you decide.

Does it replace my staff?

No. It does the drafting work: the recaps, the first drafts, the research and the reminders. Your people review, decide and build relationships.

What do I need to buy?

A Mac mini (about $600 to $900) and your own AI account (about $40 to $80 a month). You buy them directly, at cost, so you own them.

How long does it take?

The workflows we agree on in scoping are live within 30 days of kickoff, as long as I have timely access and you attend the setup sessions.

What happens after launch?

Full Workforce includes 30 days of hands-on support. After that, a care plan keeps your workforce updated, monitored and improving.
